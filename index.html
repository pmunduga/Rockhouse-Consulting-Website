The AERO Implementation Methodology

Eight phases, organized into four transformation modes.

Every engagement — whether the work is a shop-floor performance system or an agentic AI pilot — moves through the same discipline: understand the current state, design the future one, prove it narrowly, then keep learning. Not a one-time project. A cycle organizations return to.

Assess

Enterprise Assessment and Decision Discovery — an evidence-based read on current capability and the decisions worth transforming first.

Design

Strategic Intent, Agentic Decision Design, and Decision Governance — the future-state capability and the guardrails around it.

Implement

Governed Pilot Implementation and Enterprise Transformation — proven narrow, then scaled in sequenced waves.

Improve

Enterprise Learning & Continuous Improvement — operational evidence feeding back into every earlier phase.
