Services

Three practice areas. One methodology underneath all of them.

Every engagement runs on the AERO Implementation Methodology — a repeatable, eight-phase approach to building enterprise decision capability. It doesn't start with technology. It starts with the decisions that actually drive performance, then builds the governance and operating model around them.

Operational Excellence

Root-cause diagnostics, KPI and governance architecture, Leader Standard Work, and daily management systems — the discipline that turns a performance target into a sustained habit.

Enterprise Analytics & BI

Governed Power BI and data platforms built on real semantic models, not spreadsheets stitched together — designed so the numbers survive contact with an executive review.

Agentic AI Strategy & Governance

Decision architecture, agent design, and the governance layer — decision rights, autonomy tiers, guardrails, monitoring — that lets intelligent systems earn expanded authority without losing accountability.

The AERO Implementation Methodology

Four modes, eight phases, one continuous cycle.

The methodology is organized around four transformation modes — not a one-time project sequence, but a cycle organizations return to as strategy, governance, and operating conditions evolve.

Assess

Where are we today? An evidence-based understanding of current capability.

Design

What should we become? The future-state decision capability and operating model.

Implement

How do we get there? Controlled pilots that earn the right to scale.

Improve

How do we keep learning? Operational evidence feeding back into every earlier phase.

The eight phases

01

Strategic Intent Design — Design

Executive leadership defines why the enterprise is pursuing agentic AI, the business outcomes it should achieve, and the constraints that will govern every decision that follows — before any technology gets chosen.

Enterprise asset: Strategic Intent
02

Enterprise Assessment — Assess

An evidence-based baseline across Strategic Alignment, Technical Enablement, Decision Governance, and Operational Adoption — read as a profile, not a single blended score, so the real constraint (often governance, not technology) doesn't get hidden.

Enterprise asset: Enterprise Capability Baseline
03

Decision Discovery — Assess

Identifies and prioritizes the specific decisions that actually drive performance. Every agent ultimately exists to improve a decision, not automate a task — so decisions, not use cases, are the starting point.

Enterprise asset: Enterprise Decision Architecture
04

Agentic Decision Design — Design

Designs the decision capability itself — the functional signals it needs, what the agent may reason about and act on, and where it must stop and hand off to a person.

Enterprise asset: Enterprise Agent Portfolio
05

Decision Governance Design — Design

Governance designed alongside the capability, not bolted on after: who can decide what, at what tier of delegated authority, with what guardrails and escalation path when something falls outside them.

Enterprise asset: Enterprise Decision Governance Framework
06

Governed Pilot Implementation — Implement

A deliberately narrow, controlled deployment that validates business value and governance effectiveness under real operating conditions — and surfaces the gaps design couldn't fully anticipate.

Enterprise asset: Validated Decision Capability
07

Enterprise Transformation — Implement

Standardizes and expands the validated capability across sites or functions — in sequenced waves, not a single rollout, so lessons from one hub inform the next.

Enterprise asset: Enterprise Agentic Operating Model
08

Enterprise Learning & Continuous Improvement — Improve

Operational evidence feeds back into every earlier phase — governance tiers, decision rights, even Strategic Intent itself get revisited as the enterprise learns. Transformation as a continuous cycle, not a finished project.

Enterprise asset: Enterprise Learning System
